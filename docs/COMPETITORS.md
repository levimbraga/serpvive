## SEOTesting.com — Most Direct Data Competitor
**Pricing:** $50/site | $125/5 sites | $375/20 sites
**Trial:** 14 days free, no credit card

**Best at:** Content Decay Report (13-16 months data), SEO A/B Testing, LLM Testing, Chrome Extension, responsive founder
**Worst at:** Zero AI, no alerts, no diagnosis of WHY, no recommendations, no SERP analysis, no seasonal filter ("use your SEO knowledge"), price per site adds up fast

## Surfer SEO — The $99/mo Suite
**Pricing:** Discovery $49 | Standard $99 | Pro $182 | Peace of Mind $299
**Users:** 150K+. Acquired by Positive Group (FR) 2025.

**Best at:** Content Editor with Content Score (best-in-class), Google Docs + WordPress plugins, AI Tracker for GEO, Topical Map, Auto-Optimize, strong brand
**Worst at:** Content Audit is MANUAL (not continuous), credit system doesn't roll over, billing complaints (#1 on Trustpilot), Content Score can encourage keyword stuffing, add-ons fragment pricing, no free trial, weak non-English

## Clearscope — Premium Enterprise
**Pricing:** Essentials $189/mo (100 pages) | Business $399/mo (300 pages)
**Clients:** Adobe, Shopify, Nvidia, Deloitte

**Best at:** Semantic analysis quality (IBM Watson + Google NLP), Google Docs plugin, Content Inventory with decay detection, enterprise credibility, unlimited users, exceptional support
**Worst at:** $189 MINIMUM, only 100 pages on Essentials, only 20 reports/month, no free trial (must book demo), no diagnosis of WHY, no specific recommendations, no backlink tracking

## Frase.io — MOST DANGEROUS Feature Competitor
**Pricing:** Starter $45 | Professional $115 | Scale ~$199
**Content Opportunities:** Requires Professional ($115) or add-on

**Best at:** Content Opportunities (decay detection, launched Oct 2025), email + Slack alerts, 1-click alert to editor, dual SEO + GEO scoring, AI Search Tracking, affordable entry, 4.8/5 on G2
**Worst at:** Content Opportunities is ~6 months old, NOT on Starter ($45), chronic bug history, lifetime deal drama, AI writer mediocre, does NOT diagnose WHY, does NOT analyze SERP, does NOT generate action recommendations, does NOT measure results post-refresh

## FreshRank (Themeisle) — NEW Direct Competitor ⚠️
**Company:** Themeisle (Romania). Makers of Neve theme (millions of installs).
**Pricing:** Free plugin + Pro $49-149/year. Also freshrank.ai (free web tool).
**Platform:** WordPress plugin only

**Best at:** GSC integration with priority scoring (0-90), AI-powered content analysis (GPT-5 + OpenRouter), draft generation (AI rewrites sections), 5 analysis categories (UX, factual, SEO, AI visibility, opportunities), severity levels, side-by-side diff view, human-in-the-loop approval
**Worst at:**
- **WordPress ONLY** — excludes Webflow, Squarespace, custom sites, headless CMS
- **No Health Score** — no single number for blog health
- **No Decay Velocity** — doesn't measure speed of decline
- **No Seasonal Detection** — no false positive filtering
- **No Result Tracking** — no automatic before/after measurement
- **No SERP competitor analysis** — doesn't read competitor content from Google results
- **No cannibalization detection**
- **Requires user's own API key** — user pays OpenAI directly (confusing pra non-technical)
- **No continuous monitoring** — user must manually trigger analysis
- **No weekly digest email**
- **Content decay is feature, not core DNA** — Themeisle is a theme company, not an SEO company
