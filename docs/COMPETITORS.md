# Competitor Pricing & Features

> Last verified: March 2026. Verify quarterly by checking pricing pages.

## SerpVive (us)
- **Free:** 1 site, 100 pages, weekly monitoring, Health Score, 1 AI diagnosis
- **Starter:** $29/mo - 1 site, 100 pages, 10 AI diagnoses/month
- **Pro:** $69/mo - 3 sites, 1,000 pages, 40 AI diagnoses/month
- **Agency:** $129/mo - 10 sites, 5,000 pages, 120 AI diagnoses/month

## Semrush
- Source: https://www.semrush.com/pricing/
- **Pro:** $139.95/mo (annual: ~$117/mo)
- **Guru:** $249.95/mo (annual: ~$208/mo)
- **Business:** $499.95/mo (annual: ~$417/mo)
- Free trial: 7 days
- Content Audit: included in all plans, manual setup

## Surfer SEO

- **Medium:** $125/mo (5 sites, 75 credits)

- Note: Plans renamed. "Starter" is now "Single Site".

## Key Positioning vs Each Competitor

**vs Semrush:** All-in-one suite ($140+) vs focused decay monitoring (free). Use both: Semrush for research, SerpVive for monitoring.

**vs Surfer SEO:** Content creation ($119+) vs content protection (free). Different lifecycle stages. Write with Surfer, monitor with SerpVive.

**vs Frase:** Frase requires $129/mo Professional for Content Opportunities (detection only). SerpVive detects AND diagnoses for free.

**vs Clearscope:** Both start at $129/mo for paid tiers, but SerpVive has a free plan with real monitoring. Clearscope measures optimization quality; SerpVive measures actual traffic performance.

**vs SEOTesting:** Testing tool ($50+) vs monitoring + diagnosis tool (free). Complementary: SerpVive finds and diagnoses, SEOTesting measures statistical significance of changes.
