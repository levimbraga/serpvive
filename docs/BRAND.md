# BRAND — SerpVive

## Nome
**SerpVive** — SERP (Search Engine Results Page) + Vive (vida, reviver)
- Domínio: serpvive.com
- Pronúncia: surp-VAIV (inglês) ou serp-VEE-veh (latim)
- Significado: "Reviva seus resultados no Google"
- Verificado: zero resultados no Google, nenhum produto/trademark existente

## Positioning
SerpVive monitors your blog, detects posts losing traffic, diagnoses exactly WHY with AI, and tells you exactly WHAT to do to recover — with micro-drafts so you can write without researching. The complete content rescue system.

- **Slogan curto:** "Detect. Diagnose. Recover."
- **Dor:** "Stop losing traffic you already earned."
- **CTA waitlist:** "Be first to revive your rankings."
