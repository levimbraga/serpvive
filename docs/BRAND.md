## Positioning (Obviously Awesome framework)
**For** SEO freelancers and content marketers **who** manage blogs that depend on organic search traffic, **SerpVive** is a content decay monitor **that** detects posts losing traffic, diagnoses exactly WHY with SERP evidence, tells you exactly WHAT to update, and proves it worked. **Unlike** manual GSC audits, SEOTesting (data-only), or Surfer (manual content audits), **SerpVive** provides the complete 5-step rescue loop with AI intelligence at every step.

**Market strategy:** Big Fish, Small Pond. Own "content decay" as a category within SEO tools.

## Hero Copy (landing page)
- **H1 (loss framing):** "Your blog is losing traffic right now."
- **H2:** "SerpVive finds the dying posts, explains why, and tells you exactly what to fix."
- **Badge:** "Powered by Claude Opus 4.6 · Read-only access · Cancel anytime"
- **CTA button:** "Revive your rankings → Start free trial"
- **Secondary CTA:** "See how it works ↓"

**Why this framing:** Loss aversion is 2x more motivating than gain (Cialdini). AI in badge as authority signal, not headline (Dunford). Customer is the hero, not our technology (Miller).
